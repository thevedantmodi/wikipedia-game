How to play
  • Everyone starts on the same article and races to the same target.
  • Only links inside the article body work — no search bar, no navboxes.
  • Fastest time mode (the default) ranks by time, and the race ends 2 minutes after the first player finishes. Fewest clicks mode ranks by clicks.
  • The host can allow the back button (each back costs a click), set click or time limits, and choose whether opponents' pages are visible live.
  • Find-in-page (Ctrl/Cmd+F) is off unless the host turns it on.
  • Tip: go broad (countries, centuries, wars), then narrow toward the target's neighbours.
