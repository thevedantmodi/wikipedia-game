France is a country located primarily in Western Europe. Its capital and largest city is Paris. The territory of modern France was inhabited by Celtic peoples known as the Gauls before its conquest by the Roman Republic.

Following the French Revolution of 1789, the country was reshaped as a republic. It later became an empire under Napoleon, whose campaigns spread across Europe during the Napoleonic Wars.

France is a founding member of the European Union and the United Nations, and one of the five permanent members of the UN Security Council.
